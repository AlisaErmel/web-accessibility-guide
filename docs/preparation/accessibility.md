# Definition of Accessibility

Before diving into technical practices, it’s important to clearly understand what **accessibility** means in the context of web development.

---

## What is Accessibility?

**Accessibility** is the practice of designing and developing digital products so that **everyone can use them**, regardless of their abilities or the conditions in which they access the web.

It focuses on removing barriers and ensuring that users can **perceive**, **understand**, **navigate**, and **interact** with a web application effectively.

## Why Accessibility Matters

Accessibility is not just a technical requirement — it represents a commitment to **inclusion**.

An accessible web application:
- Provides **equal access** to information and functionality  
- Reduces friction in user interaction  
- Improves overall **usability and clarity**  

For example:
- Clear structure makes content easier to understand 📄  
- Good contrast improves readability in different environments ☀️  
- Logical navigation helps users move through the interface efficiently ⌨️  

## Who Benefits?

Accessibility benefits a wide range of users:

- People accessing content under different conditions  
- Users on various devices and screen sizes  
- Anyone experiencing temporary limitations  
- In fact, **all users**, through improved usability  

## Key Idea 💡

Accessibility is not an optional feature — it is a **core quality** of a well-designed web application.

By applying accessibility principles, you create products that are:
- More **usable**  
- More **inclusive**  
- More **robust**  

And ultimately, better for everyone. 💟
